    What is Spin the Wheel?

    Spin the Wheel is a simple decision-making tool that randomly selects one option from your list. It can be used for games, giveaways, classroom activities, choosing meals, picking tasks, or making any random decision.

    How to Use

    1. Add your options.
    2. Edit or remove them if needed.
    3. Click the wheel to spin.
    4. Wait for the wheel to stop.
    5. The winning option is displayed below the wheel.
