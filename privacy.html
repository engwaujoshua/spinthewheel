Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Spin the Wheel ("we," "our," "the site") respects your privacy. This page explains what happens when you use this website.

1. No Personal Data Collected

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. You do not need to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or any other personal details to use the wheel.

2. No Cookies or Tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics tools, or third-party tracking scripts of any kind. Nothing you do on this site is tracked or stored by us.

3. Local Storage on Your Device

Any wheel settings or options you customize (colors, labels, etc.) may be saved locally in your own browser so your wheel stays the way you left it. This data stays on your device only — it is never sent to us or stored on our servers.

4. Hosting Provider Logs

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Like virtually all web hosting providers, Cloudflare may automatically log basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) for security and performance purposes. We do not have access to this data for tracking individuals, and it is not used by us for any purpose. For more information, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

This site does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, this applies equally to visitors of all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if we add analytics or new features that involve data collection in the future), this page will be updated with a new "last updated" date.
